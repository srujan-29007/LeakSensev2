# M15: leak detection (stretch goal)

- **Generated by:** `leaksense eval --task detect`, config `final`, commit `8c7867b4ad` (dirty), data manifest `6281be1c3cf2`, seed 0.
- **Scenarios:** 419 CV scenarios (5 folds, each scored out of fold); the locked test is not used.
- **Samples:** 8253 leak-free daily windows (negatives; up to 20 per scenario, ending at least 1 day before any leak) and 487 leaks whose first 24 h is the positive window (628 leaks in these scenarios; 141 lack 14 leak-free days before them or last under 24 h).
- **Statistic:** daily window-mean ΔH against the 14 days right before it (rolling `tod_weekday` profile), scaled per junction by the training folds' leak-free spread; `max` = max over sensed junctions of −ΔH/σ, `mean` = their mean. Threshold = the (1 − α) quantile of the training folds' negative scores, per sensor set.
- **Sensor sets:** random = the harness's 20 seeded sets per k (averaged); greedy = the M11 final order. 95% CIs: 1000 bootstrap resamples over scenarios.

## Leak flow ramp check

Over 628 leaks: flow at the first step / median flow has median 1.000 (min 0.530); steps until 95% of the median: median 0, p95 1, max 2381. 588 leaks (93.6%) are at 95% of their median flow from the first step; the median coefficient of variation of a leak's flow is 0.020.

**Not every leak starts at full size** by the fixed rule (at least 95% of leaks at 95% from the first step; here 93.6%), so time to detect is reported. For the leaks that do start at full size it mostly measures how fast the window fills with leak steps.
