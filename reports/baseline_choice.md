# Baseline choice (M4)

- **Generated by:** `leaksense baseline-study --config` (full), commit `8915c323c5`, data manifest `6281be1c3cf2`, seed 0.
- **Sources:** leak-free rows only — Scenario-1 plus the pre-leak stretch of 419 non-test scenarios. The 70 locked-test scenarios were not loaded.
- **Method:** pseudo-cases split a leak-free stretch at a pseudo-start s. The baseline is the N days before s; ΔH is evaluated over the 7 days after s. The true ΔH is 0, so the RMS of window-mean ΔH is the baseline error (noise + bias).

## Decision

- Slot method / baseline days / estimator: **tod_weekday/14d/mean** (history 14-28 d: tod_weekday/14d/mean).
- Window **48** steps, stride **24**; max lag **7.0 days**.
- Leak-free noise at that method: 0.984 m per step, 0.149 m per window.
- Rules: method: lowest median window RMS at W=48; within 5%, the simpler slot/estimator, then the lower noise; window: smallest W with median RMS <= 0.2 m; stride: W/2; max_lag_days: largest lag up to which median |bias| < the lag 0-7 d RMS.
